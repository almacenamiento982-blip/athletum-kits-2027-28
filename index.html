Why Athletum

How could this benefit your organization?

Sponsoring Athletum FC places your brand on the kit our players wear every season, on and off the pitch.

01

Brand exposure & visibility

Associate your company with Athletum FC and the Juventus Academy Miami family, the largest youth soccer academy in South Florida, at MLS NEXT matches and major US tournaments.

02

Targeted marketing

Reach young soccer players and their families, an audience engaged with sports, health and lifestyle products and services.

03

Networking opportunities

Sponsorship comes with access to club events and the wider Athletum and Juventus Academy Miami community.

04

Social media presence

Your logo and name featured on the official Athletum and Juventus Academy Miami social media channels.

05

Community engagement

Supporting a youth program shows your company's commitment to community development and the next generation.
